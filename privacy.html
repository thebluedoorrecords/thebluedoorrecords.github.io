Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Blue Door Records ("we") handles data in Blue Door Esteira, our internal publishing tool, and on this website.

What Blue Door Esteira is

Blue Door Esteira is a private tool, still being built, used only by Blue Door Records to manage the label's own YouTube channels (Lino Bossa and Leo Jazz). It is designed to upload and schedule our videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, read the analytics of those same channels and help us answer comments on our own videos. Each feature asks for access to a channel only once it is in place; until then, no access is requested. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one outside the label signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

Blue Door Esteira uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

Once the corresponding feature is in place, the tool may access:

  • Basic information of our own channels (channel ID, title).
  • The videos, playlists and metadata of our own channels.
  • Aggregated analytics of our own channels (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate).
  • Public comments on our own videos, which include the commenter's public display name and the text they chose to post.

Comments are read only to answer and moderate them on our own videos. We do not collect any other personal data of viewers, we do not build profiles of commenters, and we do not access data of channels other than our own.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to publish our videos and to understand how our channels perform. Authorization tokens and reports are stored on the label's own computer and are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Comment text and commenter names are not stored. Data obtained from the YouTube API is deleted or refreshed within 30 days, and the authorization behind stored analytics is re-confirmed at least every 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, or when a stored authorization stops working, we delete the stored tokens and any API data from that channel within 7 days.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms, and it does not collect personal data.
