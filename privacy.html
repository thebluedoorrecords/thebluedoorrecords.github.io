Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Blue Door Records ("we") handles data in Blue Door Esteira, our internal publishing tool, and on this website.

What Blue Door Esteira is

Blue Door Esteira is a private tool used only by Blue Door Records to manage the label's own YouTube channels (Lino Bossa and Leo Jazz). It uploads and schedules our videos, sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads the analytics of those same channels. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the label signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

Blue Door Esteira uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

  • Basic information of our own channels (channel ID, title).
  • The videos, playlists and metadata of our own channels.
  • Aggregated analytics of our own channels (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate) and public comments on our own videos.

We do not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube user.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to publish our videos and to understand how our channels perform. Authorization tokens and reports are stored on the label's own computer and are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Analytics data obtained from the API is kept for no longer than 30 days unless refreshed, in line with YouTube's developer policies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens and any API data within 7 days.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms, and it does not collect personal data.
